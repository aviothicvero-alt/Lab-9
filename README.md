# Lab-9
Overview
This lab focuses on drafting and auditing platform-tailored marketing campaigns for aviation businesses using Generative AI. It emphasizes adapting tone, structure, and length across diverse channels—such as LinkedIn, Instagram, ad headlines, and promotional emails—while rigorously enforcing legal compliance, fact verification, and condition preservation.

Key Highlights
Multi-Platform Copy Adaptation: Translates a unified campaign brief (AeroFly Student Saver) across four channels, customizing content for LinkedIn's professional audience, Instagram's visual community, short-form ad headlines, and detailed promotional emails without altering core facts.

Non-Compliant Copy Auditing: Identifies and corrects common AI hallucination hazards in marketing copy, such as replacing absolute claims ("10% guaranteed", "free baggage", "unlimited seats") with compliant, conditional phrasing ("up to 10% on base fare", "subject to fare rules and availability").

Systematic Verification Tools: Applies structured auditing tools, including a Fact-Difference Check (verifying unchanged, missing, or altered parameters) and a Register Check (filtering out exaggerated or casual phrasing like "insane deal" in favor of professional brand language).

Original Aviation Campaign ("FlyEasy Airport App"): Develops a complete launch campaign for a fictional passenger navigation app, incorporating explicit operational boundaries (e.g., acknowledging dependencies on airport data systems) to prevent legal over-promising.

Human-in-the-Loop Compliance Routine: Demonstrates how peer feedback and strict compliance checklists catch subtle over-generalizations before final campaign publication and GitHub portfolio deployment.
